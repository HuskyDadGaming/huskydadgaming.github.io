🐲 Blackwing Lair

"Nefarian's laboratory atop Blackrock Spire, where the black dragonflight bred the chromatic experiments. WoW's second 40-man raid — home of the Tier 2 armour sets, Nefarian's head, and weapons like Ashkandi and the Chromatically Tempered Sword."

🎯 BWL Loot Summary

Equipment Focus: Tier 2 armour sets, high-end weapons, and the trinket Drake Fang Talisman.

  • Best for: Level 60 (some fire resistance for Vaelastrasz)
  • Raid size: 40 players
  • Boss levels: 63 (Elite)
  • Famous for: Tier 2 armour sets, Ashkandi, Chromatically Tempered Sword, Claw of Chromaggus, Drake Fang Talisman
  • Includes: Eight encounters from Razorgore to Nefarian, including the Razorgore orb control and Chromaggus dual-breath fights
