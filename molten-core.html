🌋 Molten Core

"The molten heart of Blackrock Mountain and lair of Ragnaros the Firelord, summoned by the Dark Iron to serve Sargeras. WoW's first 40-man raid — home of the Tier 1 armour sets, the Eye of Sulfuras, and two of the three Bindings of the Windseeker for Thunderfury."

🎯 MC Loot Summary

Equipment Focus: Tier 1 dungeon-set armour (Might / Prophecy / Nightslayer / Cenarion / Earthfury / Arcanist / Felheart / Giantstalker), fire-resistance gear for the Ragnaros push, and legendary components.

  • Best for: Level 60 (fire resistance recommended)
  • Raid size: 40 players
  • Boss levels: 63 (Elite)
  • Famous for: Tier 1 armour sets, Eye of Sulfuras (Sulfuras, Hand of Ragnaros), Bindings of the Windseeker (Thunderfury)
  • Includes: Ten bosses from Lucifron to Ragnaros, Majordomo's add gauntlet, and the multi-add Sulfuron and Golemagg fights
