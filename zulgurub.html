🐍 Zul'Gurub

"The troll city of the Gurubashi, where the Atal'ai priesthood is resurrecting the blood god Hakkar. WoW's first 20-man raid, famous for the Swift Zulian Tiger and Swift Razzashi Raptor mounts and the Zandalar reputation sets."

🎯 ZG Loot Summary

Equipment Focus: Zandalar reputation gear, enchanting coins and bijous, and two rare mounts.

  • Best for: Level 60
  • Raid size: 20 players
  • Boss levels: 63 (Elite)
  • Famous for: Swift Zulian Tiger and Swift Razzashi Raptor mounts, Bloodlord's Defender, Zin'rokh Destroyer of Worlds
  • Includes: Five animal-aspect High Priests plus Mandokir, Gahz'ranka, Jin'do and the blood god Hakkar
