🐲 Onyxia's Lair

"Broodmother of the black dragonflight and youngest child of Deathwing, retuned in patch 3.2.2 as a level-80 10/25-man raid. A single-boss smash-and-grab: bait the phase-two airborne Deep Breath, thin the Onyxian Whelp swarms pouring from the side caves, and collect a guaranteed epic — plus the Head of Onyxia, still handed in for the old city-wide Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer buff."

🎯 Onyxia Loot Summary

Equipment Focus: fast level-80 catch-up epics — off-set armour, cloaks, rings and neckpieces on par with early Naxxramas and heroic gear, plus the Head of Onyxia city-buff turn-in.

  • Best for: Level 80 (fresh-80 gearing / weekly lockout)
  • Raid size: 10 or 25 players
  • Boss level: 83 (Elite)
  • Famous for: The Head of Onyxia (Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer), fast one-boss epic loot, the phase-two Deep Breath
  • Includes: A single encounter across three phases — ground, airborne whelp phase with Deep Breath, and the enraged landing
