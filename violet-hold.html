"Dalaran's arcane prison, breached by the blue dragonflight — a defend-the-portals gauntlet where a random pair of imprisoned horrors break loose before Cyanigosa herself steps through the final portal."

🎯 VH Loot Summary

Equipment Focus: level 77 blues from a rotating boss pool — every run pulls two random wardens plus Cyanigosa, so across repeat runs the loot spans all armour types.

  • Best for: Level 76-77

  • Famous for: The randomized boss roster and the portal-defense format
  • Includes: Two of six random mini-bosses (Erekem, Moragg, Ichoron, Xevozz, Lavanthor, Zuramat) then Cyanigosa
